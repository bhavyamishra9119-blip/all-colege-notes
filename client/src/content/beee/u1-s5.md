### 8. Resonance: Series and Parallel

#### 8.1 Series Resonance (Acceptor / Voltage-Magnifier Circuit)

* **Resonance Condition:** Inductive reactance equals capacitive reactance:
$$X_L = X_C \implies 2\pi f_r L = \frac{1}{2\pi f_r C}$$
* **Resonant Frequency:**
$$f_r = \frac{1}{2\pi \sqrt{LC}} \qquad \omega_r = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}$$
* **Key Properties at Resonance:**
  * Minimum Impedance: $Z_{\min} = R$ (purely resistive).
  * Maximum Current: $I_{\max} = \frac{V}{R}$ (called an **Acceptor Circuit**, used in radio tuning).
  * Unity Power Factor: $\cos\phi = 1$ (voltage and current in phase).
  * Voltage Magnification across L and C: $V_L = V_C = Q \times V$.
* **Quality Factor ($Q$-Factor):**
$$Q = \frac{\omega_r L}{R} = \frac{1}{\omega_r C R} = \frac{1}{R}\sqrt{\frac{L}{C}}$$
* **Bandwidth (BW) & Half-Power Frequencies:**
$$\text{BW} = f_2 - f_1 = \frac{f_r}{Q} = \frac{R}{2\pi L}$$

#### 8.2 Parallel Resonance (Rejector / Tank Circuit)

* In a parallel circuit containing an inductive coil ($R, L$) in parallel with capacitor $C$:
* **Resonant Frequency:**
$$f_r = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{1}{LC} - \frac{R^2}{L^2}}$$
* **Dynamic Impedance:**
$$Z_d = \frac{L}{C R}$$
* **Key Properties:** Dynamic impedance is maximum ($Z_{\max}$), line current is minimum ($I_{\min} = \frac{V}{Z_d}$), called a **Rejector Circuit**.

### 9. Power Factor and Power Triangle

#### 9.1 Power Factor Definition & Formulas

$$\text{Power Factor (PF)} = \cos\phi = \frac{R}{Z} = \frac{\text{Active Power } P}{\text{Apparent Power } S}$$

#### 9.2 The Three AC Powers & Power Triangle

* **1. Active / Real / True Power ($P$):** Actual useful work converted into mechanical work, heat, or light:
$$P = V I \cos\phi \qquad (\text{Unit: Watt [W] / kW})$$
* **2. Reactive Power ($Q$):** Oscillates between source and reactive magnetic/electric fields (performs zero net mechanical work):
$$Q = V I \sin\phi \qquad (\text{Unit: VAR / kVAR})$$
* **3. Apparent Power ($S$):** Total vector power supplied by generator and transformer:
$$S = V I = \sqrt{P^2 + Q^2} \qquad (\text{Unit: Volt-Ampere [VA] / kVA})$$

#### 9.3 Low Power Factor Disadvantages & Industrial Correction

* **Disadvantages of Low PF:** Higher line currents ($I = \frac{P}{V \cos\phi}$), larger cable cross-section needed, higher $I^2 R$ transmission losses, and heavy utility financial penalties.
* **Methods of Power Factor Improvement:**
  1. **Static Shunt Capacitor Banks:** Connected in parallel with lagging inductive loads to inject leading reactive VARs ($Q_c = P(\tan\phi_1 - \tan\phi_2)$).
  2. **Synchronous Condenser:** Over-excited unloaded synchronous motor drawing leading current.

### 10. Industrial Applications of DC and AC Circuits

#### 10.1 Major Industrial Applications of DC Circuits

* **1. Electrochemistry, Electroplating & Metal Refining:** Pure unidirectional DC is mandatory for electroplating (chrome, gold, zinc) and aluminum/copper electrolytic extraction.
* **2. High-Torque Variable Speed Drives & Electric Traction:** DC series motors deliver massive starting torque for subway trains, mine hoists, and steel rolling mills.
* **3. Battery Storage & Electric Vehicles (EVs):** High-voltage DC battery packs in EVs and microgrids require DC fast chargers.
* **4. High-Voltage DC (HVDC) Bulk Transmission:** Transmits gigawatts over ultra-long distances without capacitive dielectric losses.
* **5. Industrial Automation & DC Arc Welding:** Factory PLCs and sensors operate on stable $24\text{ V}$ DC busbars.

#### 10.2 Major Industrial Applications of AC Circuits

* **1. Bulk Power Generation & National Grid Transmission:** Stepped up via transformers to $400\text{ kV} / 765\text{ kV}$ for low-loss long-distance distribution.
* **2. Industrial AC Induction Motors:** Rugged, brushless 3-phase squirrel cage motors power 90% of industrial pumps, compressors, and factory machinery.
* **3. Industrial Heating & Metallurgy:** High-frequency AC powers induction furnaces for steel melting and heat treating.
* **4. RF Communication & Signal Filtering:** R–L–C resonant AC tank circuits tune and filter communication channels.
